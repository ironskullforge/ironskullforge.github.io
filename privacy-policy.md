# ListCheck – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

ListCheck is developed by Iron Skull Forge ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the ListCheck app for Android.

## Data we collect

**None.** ListCheck has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting SDKs. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data, and we have no servers that receive your data.

## Data stored on your device

The army lists, settings and other content you create or import are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, the system may include this data in your personal Google backup. That backup is managed by Google under your Google account, and we cannot access it.

## Internet connections

ListCheck connects to the internet only to download content you ask for:

- Game data files from public GitHub repositories (github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com).
- Army lists from links you paste or open in the app (for example 40k.app, battlebase.app, listhammer.info, yellowscribe.link).
- Reference pages you choose to open (for example wahapedia.ru, 39k.pro).

These requests are made directly from your device to those websites. Like any website, they may receive standard technical information such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply to them.

## Images

If you save or share an image of a list, the app writes it to your gallery or hands it to the app you choose to share with. This happens only when you ask for it.

## Children

ListCheck is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a new date.
